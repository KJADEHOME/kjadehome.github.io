Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the MOQ for custom ceramic mugs?

MOQ is 500 pieces per design. You can mix colours within the same design. Volume discounts apply from 3,000 pcs.

Q: Can you print my logo or artwork on the mugs?

Yes. Send vector artwork (AI/PDF) and we will produce a print-proof sample. Inside, outside, full-wrap and handle prints are all available.

Q: How are mugs packed for export?

Standard export packing is bubble wrap plus master carton with drop-test verification. Retail-ready gift boxes and sleeves are available for an extra cost.

Q: Are your mugs food-safe and compliant?

Yes. Food-contact glazes are used and we can supply FDA, Prop 65 and LFGB test reports from third-party labs.
