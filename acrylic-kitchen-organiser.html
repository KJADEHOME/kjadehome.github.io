Frequently Asked Questions

Is the acrylic clear enough for retail display?

We use high-transmission PMMA for display-grade clarity. Frosted and tinted variants are available when you want a softer look.

Can these bins go in the dishwasher?

Hand wash is recommended. Acrylic withstands low-temperature wash cycles but can cloud under prolonged high heat.

What is the MOQ for a custom size?

MOQ is 500 pieces per size using existing moulds. New mould tooling is quoted separately.

Do you pack sets for retail?

Yes. Sets are assembled with inserts and packed into retail cartons with your barcode and labelling.
