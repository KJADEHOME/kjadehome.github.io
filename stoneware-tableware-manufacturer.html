Frequently Asked Questions

Are your bowls microwave and dishwasher safe?

Yes. Bothstoneware and porcelain bodies are fired to vitrified levels and are tested for microwave and dishwasher use. Test reports are available on request.

What is the MOQ per design?

MOQ is 500 pieces per design, and you can mix sizes within the same design. Dinner set components can be mixed in one order.

Can we develop a proprietary shape?

Yes. New mould development is quoted separately depending on the body type and annual volume commitment.

How do you pack fragile tableware for export?

Each piece gets an individual foam sleeve, then a double-wall master carton. We run drop tests on the carton before bulk production.
